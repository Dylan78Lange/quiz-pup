# 🐶 Quiz Pup

A 90s-style quiz show where your pup is the star! Answer questions, win coins, and watch your
pup dance (or get up to mischief when you're wrong).

**▶ Play it here: https://dylan78lange.github.io/quiz-pup/**

- 1 to 4 players taking turns on one computer
- 6 themes and 300 questions: Geography, Video Games, Kids' Movies, Food, Brand Icons and Famous People
- Easy, Medium and Hard levels, 3 lives, and coins to bank or spend in the Pup Shop
- Pick your pup (Shiba Inu, Pug, Husky, Poodle or Dalmatian) and your host (human or robot)
- Hip hop beat and sound effects, all made with code

Made by a kid learning to code, with help from Claude Code.

Logo shapes in the Brand Icons questions come from [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org) (CC0).
